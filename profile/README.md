Topics of research include

| [🤖 **SyRI**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/research-teams/syri-robotic-systems-in-interaction/) | [🧠 **CID**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/research-teams/cid-knowledge-uncertainty-data/) | [🛡️ **SCOP**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/research-teams/scop-dependability-communication-optimization/) |
|-|-|-|
| Study the estimation, decision making and control of robotic systems in the case of robot-robot or robot-human cooperation. | Statistical learning, uncertainty management, and knowledge engineering, including work on trusted/robust AI. | Operational safety (dependability), security, optimization, and the interactions between them. |

Backed by technological platforms:

| [🚗 **Intelligent vehicles**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/technological-platforms/intelligent-vehicles/) | [🛸 **Robotics**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/technological-platforms/robotic/)  | [🕶️ **Immersive simulation**](https://www.hds.utc.fr/en/research/technological-platforms/immersive-simulation/) |
|-|-|-|
| Fleet of instrumented vehicles | Small mobile robots and aerial drones | 4-wall CAVE plus an autonomous-driving and railway-supervision simulator |
